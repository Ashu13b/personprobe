## Method & caveats

- The subject prints variously as **Prem Singh Yadav / Prem S. Yadav / P. S. Yadav / PS Yadav**. Matching treated these as one identity; `S. P. Yadav`, `A. S. Yadav`, `R. P. Yadav`, `Pankaj Yadav`, `Usha Yadav`, `Sarita Yadav`, `Preeti Yadav` and `S.K.S. Yadav` were treated as **other people**.

- Citation totals differ by source because of different indexing breadth (Crossref counts references to the DOI; OpenAlex and S2 add preprints/books and have different dedup rules).
